## Limits and exclusions

- Cato Neimoidia is atmospheric combat in the opening sequence; no surface-arrival badge is supplied.
- Republic-side Coruscant scenes and the Dooku-side Serenno scene are distinguished from remote hologram participants.
- Devaron in Witches of the Mist is a surveillance recording of an earlier attack, so it is not added as an unqualified present visit. Monster has a separately evidenced present Devaron scene.
- Four Clone Wars titles still lack named-world links in the synced registry. This review does not fabricate a named world for the unnamed moon in Victory and Death or convert a nebula/space-station setting into a planet.
- Entries carry no intra-episode sequence number, travel duration, or new coordinate. Array order is not an itinerary.
