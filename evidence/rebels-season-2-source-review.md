## Coverage boundaries

- The Siege of Lothal: physical Lothal scenes; Mustafar is recalled.
- Lost Commanders / Relics: Seelos surface arrival and confrontation; no Abafar or other clone-biography joins.
- Always Two There Are: the abandoned Republic medical station is typed as a station with a descriptive identity; its proper name and planetary anchor are unresolved.
- Brothers of the Broken Horn: Vizago’s ship is typed as a ship. Rinn/Garel supply discussions and recalled Lothal history are not visits. Azmorigan’s rendezvous geography remains unresolved.
- Wings of the Master: Shantipole surface visit and Ibaar blockade operation; no Ibaar surface landing asserted.
- Blood Sisters: Shadow Caster boarding is verified. The opening port and final rendezvous remain unjoined pending exact named-setting evidence. Havoc Outpost is an assigned destination, not sufficient proof of arrival.
- Stealth Strike: Titus’s Interdictor is a descriptive ship identity; no planetary anchor or proper ship name inferred.
- Future of the Force: Garel meeting and Takobo arrival are verified; intercepted Mustafar transmissions are not a visit.
- Legacy / A Princess on Lothal: physical locations are separated from visions and Alderaan references.
- Protector of Concord Dawn: system space and the third moon are separate identities. Neither becomes a Concord Dawn planet surface visit.
- Legends of the Lasat: arrival and Zeb’s descent to Lira San are verified; Lasan destruction is recalled. The intervening nebula has no sourced proper name in the reviewed gallery.
- The Call: gallery captions 8 and 26 establish a refinery planetoid/asteroid but no stable named-world identity. Recorded as a reviewed gap, not zero locations.
- Homecoming: Ryloth association is orbital carrier action.
- The Honorable Ones: Geonosis orbital module and its icy moon are distinct; the moon’s proper name is unverified here. Lasan history is excluded.
- Shroud of Darkness: physical Lothal temple entry is distinct from visions, voices, and the instruction to find Malachor.
- The Forgotten Droid: gallery caption 26 establishes an unnamed safe-planet arrival. No identity is inferred solely from the adjacent episode. Yost is rejected as a trap; Ryloth is recalled. Recorded as a reviewed gap.
- Mystery of Chopper Base: caption 11 names Atollon directly.
- Twilight of the Apprentice: combined gallery verifies Malachor landing and Atollon return; no part-specific split is invented.

No coordinates or travel sequence numbers are added. New location identities remain textual where independently sourced map placement is absent. All 76 mapped destinations are preserved.
