The dossiers can appear for a directory-selected textual world even when that world has no supported map position.

The artwork is original resolution-independent SVG: invented shorelines on Scarif, pale desert relief on Jedha, rocky/cloud motifs on Eadu, mineral-colored land on Lah’mu, static lava fissures on Mustafar, and vegetation shapes on Yavin 4. These are illustrative studies, not official images, surface maps, or evidence for precise geography. No imagery from the reference pages was downloaded or republished. Ring of Kafrene is excluded from planet dossiers; its asteroid-outpost identity belongs to the separate location registry.

The dossier regression suite passes 13 dossiers and 30 source pages, retaining moon, climate, installation-era, and non-planet exclusions. Browser/production validation is recorded separately for the integrated release.
