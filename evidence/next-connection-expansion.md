## Boundaries

- Quell is atmospheric action; no surface or conventional-orbit context is assigned. Ringo Vinda is explicitly orbital station/cruiser activity.
- Raxus is retained under the source name; it is not silently merged with Raxus Prime.
- Coruscant joins distinguish physical Council/Sith scenes from the remotely projected participants. Sacrifice is anchored to real Sith scenes, not only Yoda's induced vision.
- The Zillo Beast gallery only orders future transport to Coruscant, so no new Coruscant link is added for that episode.
- Assassin dialogue recalls Florrum and Naboo history; those recalled names are not added as appearances.
- Kidnapped only describes an upcoming Zygerria auction; no Zygerria visit is added to that episode. Escape from Kadavo has explicit Zygerria scenes and receives the link.
- Andor and Mandalorian highlights reviewed in this pass did not establish additional sufficiently explicit named-world scenes without broader location inference; no additions were forced.
- No coordinates, within-title sequence values, character itinerary, global chronology, or complete-scene-coverage claim is added.
