## Limits and conflicts

- Faster establishes Safa Toma Raceway; no parent world is inferred. Entombed calls Skara Nal both a remote world (caption 3) and ancient structure (caption 5). The atlas conservatively registers the structure as a surface site, without resolving the ambiguous planet name.
- Tribe names Ordo Tera spaceport; its physical/parent identity requires further review and is omitted. Kashyyyk is directly established by caption 6.
- Cid’s parlor scenes not explicitly naming Ord Mantell are not expanded by proximity or preceding episodes. The Solitary Clone barracks scene is likewise not assigned a planet by assumption.
- Metamorphosis leaves the crash-world unnamed in reviewed captions. Its Wayland caption calls the world icy; the association uses the named facility setting but does not propagate the terrain claim. Outpost spells the same facility’s world Weyland, normalized to existing Wayland ID.
- Balmorra in Tipping Point is an orbital rescue aboard a cruiser, not a surface visit. Wayland scenes follow parallel Imperial/prisoner storylines; joins do not imply that all squad members travel together.
- Pabu in Plan 99 is a retirement proposal interrupted before arrival, so it is not joined. Kamino references, recovered historical evidence and recollections are not current surface appearances. Unnamed markets, ships and incidental settings remain omitted pending identity review.
- Narrative collection order is whole-episode order; no exact dates, within-episode order, travel geometry or inferred routes are asserted.
