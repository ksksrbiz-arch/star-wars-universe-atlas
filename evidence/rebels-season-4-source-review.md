## Identity and scene boundaries

- Heroes of Mandalore: exact guide sends the rescue to Sabine’s home world; gallery documents ground rescue and later ship action.
- In the Name of the Rebellion: Yavin 4 arrival is explicit. Relay mission captions 2–9 are paired with the guide and [Brom Titus identity](https://www.starwars.com/databank/brom-titus) to name Jalindi Station. It is retained as a surface facility without invented parent coordinates. Faos is an [orbital outpost](https://www.starwars.com/databank/faos-station), identified by loading/infiltration sequence captions 11–12. Freighter 2716 is separately typed as a ship; no unknown destination is assigned.
- Flight of the Defender: gallery identifies the prototype theft/test facility; [TIE defender elite](https://www.starwars.com/databank/tie-defender-elite) places that prototype on Lothal. This combines exact event evidence with location identity, not a species-origin inference from the loth-cats.
- Crawler Commandeers: Yavin meeting is explicit; [Vizago biography](https://www.starwars.com/databank/cikatro-vizago) identifies the specific crawler enslavement/rescue on Lothal.
- Kindred: movement between Lothal hemispheres is not an interplanetary route.
- Wolves and a Door / A World Between Worlds: Lothal temple exterior is distinct from the portal realm. Mortis imagery does not create a Mortis visit. Malachor is witnessed through a portal; no Ezra surface visit is assigned. Palpatine’s remote presence does not establish Coruscant.
- A Fool’s Hope: Joopa Base rendezvous is linked to Seelos using the [named base entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/joopa-base). The mobile AT-AT identity is kept distinct from the older clone AT-TE.
- Finale: main liberation takes place on Lothal. Captions 29–30 explicitly shift to a later epilogue and recount Zeb taking Kallus to Lira San. That association is labeled as a time jump and not the crew’s next destination. Endor participation is narrated, so no new Endor appearance is inferred. The purrgil departure has no verified destination in this episode; no Peridea join is inferred.

No new coordinates or hyperspace lanes are added. Stations, ships, surface facilities and a non-geographic realm retain their own types. The 76 mapped destinations remain unchanged.
