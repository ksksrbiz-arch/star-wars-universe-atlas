## Named-location identity corroboration

Three joins combine exact episode-location evidence with an official identity anchor: Secret Weapons names the Jedi Temple war room; the [Temple entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/jedi-temple) locates the temple on Coruscant. Fugitive explicitly names Tipoca City; its [entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/tipoca-city) calls it “The central hub of Kamino”. Dangerous Debt explicitly depicts the Pyke prison/complex and indexes Pyke Stronghold; its [entry](https://www.starwars.com/databank/pyke-stronghold) says “Located on the Outer Rim world of Oba Diah”. These are location identity joins, not broad series appearances.

Destiny’s gallery describes Yoda landing on the nebula world, and its [exact episode guide](https://www.starwars.com/series/clone-wars/destiny-episode-guide) indexes “Force Planet”, described as “not recognized with any specific name”. The registry uses a descriptive unnamed identity, not a fabricated proper name or map position. Mortis remains a mystical setting without conventional coordinates.
