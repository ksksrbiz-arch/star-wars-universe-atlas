## Additional identity evidence

The exact [Replacements highlights](https://www.starwars.com/news/bad-batch-declassified-replacements) name the crash site as Ordo Moon. [Pantora’s Databank](https://www.starwars.com/databank/pantora) explicitly identifies it as a moon orbiting Orto Plutonia. Neither becomes an appearance on its parent planet.

The official [Rise of the Empire retrospective](https://www.starwars.com/news/bad-batch-empire) explicitly names Bora Vio in Bounty Lost and Nala Se’s final season-one destination as Wayland. These are retrospective confirmations of exact scenes, not guesses from broad series appearances. [Into the Breach trivia](https://www.starwars.com/into-the-breach-trivia-gallery-star-wars-the-bad-batch) independently states that Bora Vio’s facility was first seen in season-one Bounty Lost. The original episode galleries alone do not name those worlds.
