## Premiere and provider normalization

[Official series index](https://www.starwars.com/series/star-wars-rebels) numbers the regular season after the two-part premiere from S1E3. [The official anniversary](https://www.starwars.com/news/star-wars-rebels-turns-10) dates the two-episode premiere broadcast to October 3, 2014. TVmaze carries the combined premiere as a special, dated September 29, and regular installments numbered 1–13. The snapshot retains that raw provider distinction. The atlas uses one Spark of Rebellion watch record S01E01–02 and regular episode numbers 3–15. This matches the combined official premiere story gallery without inventing part-specific scene assignments or duplicate progress. Four promotional shorts remain excluded separately from the original nine excluded specials. Runtime is withheld for all records. No exact interleaving with Andor or BBY/ABY chronology is asserted.

## Boundaries and unresolved settings

- Spark: Lothal and Kessel are physical settings. Wookiee identity does not establish a Kashyyyk visit.
- Droids: the Garel-bound shuttle and subsequent Bay 7 sequence are read together; Lothal arrival is explicit. Recounted Lasan history is excluded.
- Fighter Flight: the local market/fruit context establishes Lothal; no off-world fruit origin is assigned.
- Rise of the Old Masters: the gallery establishes the Spire in the Stygeon system, while the official Stygeon Prime Databank establishes the prison planet identity. Lothal return is explicit.
- Breaking Ranks: Lothal academy is explicit. The spaceborne kyber convoy has no named planet anchor; recalled Stygeon encounter is not a new visit.
- Out of Darkness and Gathering Forces: Fort Anaxes is an asteroid outpost, separate from intact Anaxes. The initial unnamed terrain/pursuit and hyperspace segments do not become guessed planet joins.
- Path of the Jedi: physical Lothal temple entry is separate from visions and remote voices.
- Idiot’s Array: Lothal blockade/property sequence is linked; Merchant One is separately typed as a ship, without a fixed coordinate.
- Vision of Hope: Lothal training is explicit; the vision adds no separate physical planet.
- Call to Action: Lothal is explicit; other worlds receiving the broadcast remain unnamed/unresolved.
- Rebel Resolve: Lothal and Kanan’s current imprisonment aboard Sovereign are explicit. Mustafar is a destination report, not a verified arrival.
- Fire Across the Galaxy: Lothal freighter theft and Mustafar fleet arrival are explicit. Rescue aboard a Star Destroyer is represented as orbital, not a surface landing.

Lothal, Kessel and Mustafar use existing independently sourced map positions. Garel, Stygeon Prime, Fort Anaxes and both ships remain textual destinations. No new coordinates, hyperspace lanes or within-episode route ordering are added. Ships and unnamed space are not silently converted to planets.
