# Rebels season two setting dossiers

Reviewed November 7, 2026. Three broad setting files are separate from episode appearance evidence and map placement.

- [Atollon](https://www.starwars.com/databank/atollon) supports the rocky world; [krykna](https://www.starwars.com/databank/krykna) and [dokma](https://www.starwars.com/databank/dokma) support its native habitats. The guide omits the base's later outcome.
- [Malachor](https://www.starwars.com/databank/malachor) supports the ancient temple and conflict. [Dave Filoni's production-design interview](https://www.starwars.com/news/fates-fulfilled-dave-filoni-reflects-on-star-wars-rebels-season-two-part-2) explicitly describes the dark surface, underground cavern and stepped temple. It informs a clearly illustrative motif rather than asserting measured geology or architecture. The guide omits visitors' outcomes and does not merge other-continuity namesakes.
- [Seelos](https://www.starwars.com/databank/seelos) supports salt plains and distant blue mountains; [joopa](https://www.starwars.com/databank/joopa) supports subsurface habitat. Mobile walker residences are not assigned permanent coordinates. Joopa Base is not conflated with an AT-TE: its own official entry identifies a repurposed AT-AT, so the dossier stays with the broader verified landscape and mobile-travel description.

Three original SVG studies add warm rock formations, a subdued temple motif, and pale plains with blue ridges. No external image or new dependency is imported. Existing reduced-motion, accessible labels and illustrative captions remain in use. No new coordinate or route is inferred. Concord Dawn was deliberately not selected for a planet dossier because the official entry describes a system and the Protectors Camp is specifically on its third moon.
