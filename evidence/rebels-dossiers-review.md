# Rebels setting dossiers

Reviewed November 7, 2026. These are broad environmental references, separate from the episode-specific appearance evidence.

## Sources and scope

- [Lothal](https://www.starwars.com/databank/lothal): temperate plains, farms, city and era-qualified industrial occupation. [Transmitter tower](https://www.starwars.com/databank/lothal-transmitter-tower) supports a named locality without copying its later story outcomes.
- [Kessel](https://www.starwars.com/databank/kessel): hot, noxious mining world with caverns. [Miners](https://www.starwars.com/databank/kessel-miners) supports broad working conditions, not a particular Rebels episode or mine layout.
- [Stygeon Prime](https://www.starwars.com/databank/stygeon-prime): cold mountains and native tibidees. [The Spire](https://www.starwars.com/databank/the-spire) supports the fortified prison setting; its mission outcome is omitted from the dossier.
- [Fort Anaxes](https://www.starwars.com/databank/fort-anaxes): the Rebels-era surviving outpost is distinguished from the intact Clone Wars-era world. [Fyrnock](https://www.starwars.com/databank/fyrnock) supports the site's creature habitat.

Original SVG studies distinguish Lothal's fields, Kessel's excavated terrain, Stygeon's ridges and the Fort Anaxes rock/base silhouette. They are not official artwork, surface maps or reconstructed architecture. No texture, still, external image or new animation dependency was imported. Fort Anaxes uses a static outpost study instead of a planetary globe. No coordinates, dimensions, orbital parameters or travel route are inferred.

Directory terrain filters gain plains, caverns and asteroid categories, based only on reviewed dossiers. Lothal retains its independently sourced mapped placement; Kessel has a separately reviewed T-10 placement. Stygeon Prime and Fort Anaxes remain unplaced. These environmental dossiers do not establish any of those coordinates.
