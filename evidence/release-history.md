# Release history

Canonical, deduplicated record of the per-batch catalog, sequence and association releases. `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `context/CONTEXT.md` and the context references point here instead of repeating these paragraphs. Each entry states what that batch added; counts are as of the batch, and the current totals live in [context/CONTEXT.md](../context/CONTEXT.md). Batches are listed in release order. This file records scope and caveats; it is not browser or deployment evidence, which lives in the linked per-batch reviews and `evidence/deployment/`.

## Earlier batches

Ahsoka season one adds eight separately sourced metadata records and 14 episode/world associations. The original 185-item audit remains distinct. Provider dates are preserved; unknown runtimes remain withheld. See [Ahsoka source review](ahsoka-source-review.md).

The Book of Boba Fett adds seven chapters and eleven partial associations, including historical Mandalore/Coruscant scenes, the unmapped Glavis station, and an unnamed-parent Jedi-school site. See [Boba evidence review](boba-fett-source-review.md). No new galaxy coordinates are asserted.

The prequel trilogy adds three films and twenty partial associations, including Order 66 montage contexts. Polis Massa is an asteroid outpost, and Dagobah is excluded because its Episode III arrival is a deleted scene. No map coordinates are added. See [prequel source review](prequel-source-review.md).

## Star Wars Rebels

Rebels season one adds 14 watch records covering all 15 official parts: the two-part Spark of Rebellion premiere is one progress record (S01E01–02), followed by S01E03–15. Four Rebels promotional shorts are excluded separately from the original nine excluded specials. Twenty sourced associations cover all 14 records, without claiming exhaustive scene coverage. Fort Anaxes remains an asteroid outpost; Merchant One and Sovereign remain ships; the finale’s Mustafar setting is orbital. See [Rebels source review](rebels-source-review.md).

Rebels season two adds 20 watch records covering 22 official parts, extending the existing Rebels sequence to 34 records across seasons 1–2. The Siege of Lothal (S02E01–02) and Twilight of the Apprentice (S02E21–22) retain combined progress records. Twenty-four associations cover 18 of the 20 new records; The Call and The Forgotten Droid retain explicit identity-evidence gaps. The Ultimate Guide promotional special is excluded separately. No mapped coordinates are added. See [Rebels season-two source review](rebels-season-2-source-review.md) for normalized numbering, exact gallery evidence, exclusions, the reviewed sequence extension and evidence gaps.

Rebels season three adds 19 watch records covering 22 official parts: Steps Into Shadow (1–2), Ghosts of Geonosis (12–13) and Zero Hour (21–22) share combined progress records. Its 24 reviewed associations cover all 19 records at least once, with partial scene coverage and documented source conflicts. The existing Rebels sequence preserves its 34-ID prefix and extends to 53 records. No map coordinates are added; Season 2 Recap is excluded. See [Rebels season-three source review](rebels-season-3-source-review.md).

Rebels season four adds 13 watch records covering its 15 Disney+ numbered slots. Heroes of Mandalore (1–2) and In the Name of the Rebellion (3–4) share progress records; the extended finale remains E15 with no invented E16. Twenty-one sourced associations cover all 13 records at least once, with partial scene coverage and an explicitly later Lira San epilogue. World Between Worlds is a non-geographic realm, not a planet. The Rebels sequence preserves its 53-ID prefix and contains 66 records across all four seasons. No map coordinates are added. See [Rebels season-four source review](rebels-season-4-source-review.md).

## The Bad Batch

The Bad Batch season one adds 16 consecutive episode records, a separate tenth collection sequence and 25 sourced associations covering all 16 episodes at least once. Pantora and Ordo Moon retain moon identities. Bora Vio and the finale’s Wayland destination have explicit retrospective official confirmation; Wayland is Nala Se’s parallel storyline, not a squad journey. Tipoca City is destroyed, not all of Kamino. No coordinates or exact Order 66 scene interleaving are added. Season two and season three are covered in the sections below. See [season-one source review](bad-batch-season-1-source-review.md).

The Bad Batch season two adds 16 individually numbered records and 26 reviewed associations covering every new episode at least once. Its collection extends to 32 records, preserving the season-one prefix; the other nine sequences are unchanged by this batch. Safa Toma Raceway and Skara Nal are surface sites without inferred parent planets; Balmorra is an orbital association. Wayland/Weyland spelling is normalized without adopting the conflicting ice-planet descriptor. No map coordinates are added. See [source review](bad-batch-season-2-source-review.md).

The Bad Batch season three adds 15 individually numbered episodes and 21 reviewed associations, covering every new episode at least once. The collection extends to 47 records while preserving its 32-ID prefix and the other nine sequences. Exact official episode synopses and episode highlights support appearances; the separate Mount Tantiss Databank establishes Wayland as its parent world. Suria space station is not a Suria surface visit. The finale retirement and later epilogue remain qualified, without an invented date or viewing-order change. No coordinates are added. See [source review](bad-batch-season-3-source-review.md).

Obi-Wan Kenobi adds six Part I–VI records, a separate eleventh collection and 12 reviewed associations. Part I Coruscant is a flashback; Nur is a moon distinct from Mustafar. Unnamed finale terrain, unreviewed dream/training localities and mentioned Path bases remain unjoined. A Jedi’s Return is an excluded behind-the-scenes special. All ten prior sequences are unchanged; no coordinates are added. See [Obi-Wan source review](obi-wan-source-review.md).

- Solo adds one film and seven partial associations (six planet settings and First Light ship), with twelve total collection sequences. U.S. theatrical May25 release is sourced separately from the landing-page May23 discrepancy; runtime remains withheld. See [Solo source review](solo-source-review.md) and [release verification](solo-release/verification.md).


The sequel trilogy adds three film records, a thirteenth collection and 19 reviewed associations. Kef Bir is a moon, Supremacy a ship and Hosnian Prime a destruction setting. Force projections do not establish physical arrivals; Ajan Kloss is conservatively represented by its base locality. TLJ/TROS domestic opening dates are distinguished from earlier international rollouts. All twelve previous sequences remain exact; no coordinates are added. See [sequel source review](sequel-source-review.md).

The galaxy immersion overhaul adds original layered stellar clouds and dust, 76 illustrative 3D globes, Immersive/Reference presentations and inspection without arrival. Geography, catalog and database publication remain unchanged. Mobile controls and dense labels are checked separately; see [visual overhaul verification](galaxy-immersion/verification.md).

The hyperspace overhaul replaces short chart interpolation with a4.2-second departure, perspective cloud-tunnel flight and close destination approach. Geographic context stays in an inset; Skip/Cancel and instant reduced-motion arrival remain. No factual data is changed. See [hyperspace verification](hyperspace-flight/verification.md).
