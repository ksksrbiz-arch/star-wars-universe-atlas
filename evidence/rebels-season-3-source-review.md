- Imperial Supercommandos guide says Concord Dawn, as do gallery captions 2 and 6, but gallery caption 3 labels the shattered world Mandalore. A qualified Concord Dawn system association is retained; neither a Mandalore surface join nor a third-moon join is inferred from this conflict.
- The Antilles Extraction gallery spells Atollon as Atollan in caption 10. Identity is normalized with the typo disclosed in the connection note.
