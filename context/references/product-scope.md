# Product scope

Movies and shows first. The local prototype has 76 schematic destinations: eight general worlds, 67 additional direct system-grid placements, and Endor’s forest moon using a documented parent-system join. It combines galaxy navigation, cinematic travel, dossiers, a film/episode library, release timeline, collection viewing sequences, partial story/location relationships, and browser-local progress.

The bounded catalog contains 323 items: three original-trilogy films, Andor's 24 episodes, The Clone Wars (2008)'s 133 episodes and film, The Mandalorian's 24 episodes, Ahsoka season one's eight episodes, Rogue One, The Book of Boba Fett's seven chapters, three prequel films, and Rebels seasons 1–4 (66 watch records, with combined-story and finale-numbering caveats), and The Bad Batch seasons one through three’s 47 episodes, and Obi-Wan Kenobi’s six parts. The original 185-item audit cohort is preserved separately from the Ahsoka source snapshot. The original nine promotional/Legacy specials, four Rebels introductory shorts The Ultimate Guide and Season 2 Recap are excluded. The 2003 Clone Wars series, The Mandalorian and Grogu film, books, comics, games, and Legends are outside this slice.

The ten collection sequences cover all 323 catalog items. Clone Wars follows the official chronological table; other collections preserve film/episode narrative order with flashback caveats. No globally exact event calendar is claimed. Rogue One has a separately sourced film record and sequence. The Book of Boba Fett has seven separately sourced chapters; chapters 5–7 supply a playable cross-series bridge. The prequel trilogy adds three separately sourced films. Revenge of the Sith is included in the Clone Wars overlap annotation without asserting a scene-interleaving order.

The larger vision connects time, story, geography, and people, but catalog/sequence completeness does not imply appearance completeness. Unjoined stories have no verified relationship in this atlas; reviewed gaps are recorded separately and do not establish absence of locations. Runtime, release-type, geographic and source caveats remain visible. Decorative navigation is not a canonical hyperspace route; original procedural art is not official imagery. Browser evidence applies to the source revision actually tested.


Rebels season one adds 14 watch records covering all 15 official parts: the two-part Spark of Rebellion premiere is one progress record (S01E01–02), followed by S01E03–15. Four Rebels promotional shorts are excluded separately from the original nine excluded specials. Twenty sourced associations cover all 14 records, without claiming exhaustive scene coverage. Fort Anaxes remains an asteroid outpost; Merchant One and Sovereign remain ships; the finale’s Mustafar setting is orbital. See [Rebels source review](../../evidence/rebels-source-review.md).

Rebels season two adds 20 watch records covering 22 official parts, extending the existing Rebels sequence to 34 records across seasons 1–2. The Siege of Lothal (S02E01–02) and Twilight of the Apprentice (S02E21–22) retain combined progress records. Twenty-four associations cover 18 of the 20 new records; The Call and The Forgotten Droid retain explicit identity-evidence gaps. The Ultimate Guide promotional special is excluded separately. No mapped coordinates are added.

See [season-two source review](../../evidence/rebels-season-2-source-review.md) for the exact sequence extension and evidence gaps.

Rebels season three adds 19 watch records covering 22 official parts: Steps Into Shadow (1–2), Ghosts of Geonosis (12–13) and Zero Hour (21–22) share combined progress records. Its 24 reviewed associations cover all 19 records at least once, with partial scene coverage and documented source conflicts. The existing Rebels sequence preserves its 34-ID prefix and extends to 53 records. No map coordinates are added; Season 2 Recap is excluded.

Rebels season four adds 13 watch records covering its 15 Disney+ numbered slots. Heroes of Mandalore (1–2) and In the Name of the Rebellion (3–4) share progress records; the extended finale remains E15 with no invented E16. Twenty-one sourced associations cover all 13 records at least once, with partial scene coverage and an explicitly later Lira San epilogue. World Between Worlds is a non-geographic realm, not a planet. The Rebels sequence preserves its 53-ID prefix and now contains 66 records across all four seasons. No map coordinates are added.

The Bad Batch season one adds 16 consecutive episode records, a separate tenth collection sequence and 25 sourced associations covering all 16 episodes at least once. Pantora and Ordo Moon retain moon identities. Bora Vio and the finale’s Wayland destination have explicit retrospective official confirmation; Wayland is Nala Se’s parallel storyline, not a squad journey. Tipoca City is destroyed, not all of Kamino. Season three is included by the subsequent batch below; no coordinates or exact Order 66 scene interleaving are added.

The Bad Batch season three adds 15 individually numbered episodes and 21 reviewed associations, covering every new episode at least once. See [source review](../../evidence/bad-batch-season-3-source-review.md).
