# Product scope

Movies and shows first. The local prototype has 62 schematic destinations: eight general worlds, 53 additional direct system-grid placements, and Endor’s forest moon using a documented parent-system join. It combines galaxy navigation, cinematic travel, dossiers, a film/episode library, release timeline, collection viewing sequences, partial story/location relationships, and browser-local progress.

The bounded catalog contains 185 items: three original-trilogy films, Andor's 24 episodes, The Clone Wars (2008)'s 133 episodes and film, and The Mandalorian's 24 episodes. Nine promotional/Legacy specials are excluded. The 2003 Clone Wars series, The Mandalorian and Grogu film, books, comics, games, and Legends are outside this slice.

The four collection sequences cover all 185 catalog items. Clone Wars follows the official chronological table; other collections preserve film/episode narrative order with flashback caveats. No globally exact event calendar is claimed. Rogue One, Book of Boba Fett chapters 5–7, and Revenge of the Sith overlap remain outside-collection references, not extra progress records.

The larger vision connects time, story, geography, and people, but catalog/sequence completeness does not imply appearance completeness. Unjoined stories are unreviewed, not evidence of no locations. Runtime, release-type, geographic and source caveats remain visible. Decorative navigation is not a canonical hyperspace route; original procedural art is not official imagery. Browser evidence applies to the source revision actually tested.
