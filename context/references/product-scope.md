# Product scope

Movies and shows first. The local prototype has 76 schematic destinations: eight general worlds, 67 additional direct system-grid placements, and Endor’s forest moon using a documented parent-system join. It combines galaxy navigation, cinematic travel, dossiers, a film/episode library, release timeline, collection viewing sequences, partial story/location relationships, and browser-local progress.

The bounded catalog contains 201 items: three original-trilogy films, Andor's 24 episodes, The Clone Wars (2008)'s 133 episodes and film, The Mandalorian's 24 episodes, Ahsoka season one's eight episodes, Rogue One, and The Book of Boba Fett's seven chapters. The original 185-item audit cohort is preserved separately from the Ahsoka source snapshot. Nine promotional/Legacy specials are excluded. The 2003 Clone Wars series, The Mandalorian and Grogu film, books, comics, games, and Legends are outside this slice.

The seven collection sequences cover all 201 catalog items. Clone Wars follows the official chronological table; other collections preserve film/episode narrative order with flashback caveats. No globally exact event calendar is claimed. Rogue One has a separately sourced film record and sequence. The Book of Boba Fett has seven separately sourced chapters; chapters 5–7 supply a playable cross-series bridge. Revenge of the Sith remains an outside-collection overlap reference.

The larger vision connects time, story, geography, and people, but catalog/sequence completeness does not imply appearance completeness. Unjoined stories have no verified relationship in this atlas; reviewed gaps are recorded separately and do not establish absence of locations. Runtime, release-type, geographic and source caveats remain visible. Decorative navigation is not a canonical hyperspace route; original procedural art is not official imagery. Browser evidence applies to the source revision actually tested.
