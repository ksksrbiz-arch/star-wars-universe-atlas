# Canon and source contract

[Base worlds](../../src/data.ts) and [journey worlds](../../src/journeyWorlds.ts) combine to 76 schematic destinations. The official appendix supports published system grids; setting and appearance sources support separate claims. Endor and Yavin 4 retain parent-system qualifications. A system grid does not establish an exact planet position or a story appearance.

Keep claim evidence distinct: screen appearance, parent system, published grid, and illustrative appearance are separate assertions. Published chronology must distinguish release order, story order, exact dates, and uncertain intervals. Future Canon/Legends and edition fields must not be inferred from names alone.

World descriptions are mild setting context, but the prototype is not certified spoiler-safe. Before expanding content, attach watched-title visibility rules to descriptions, relationships, thumbnails, and search results.

Conflicting or missing evidence remains explicit. Do not invent coordinates, routes, dates, or character appearances to complete the visual.

The [catalog](../../src/catalog.ts) exposes 324 release records. [Chronology](../../src/chronology.ts) separately supplies twelve whole-work viewing sequences for those IDs. Clone Wars follows its official table; Andor and Mandalorian preserve numbered narrative order, including flashbacks within their original episodes; the trilogy follows IV–V–VI. No exact BBY/ABY event calendar is asserted. The catalog’s original chronology flag describes the base release-only import, not the added sequence module.

The [relationship registry](../../src/connections.ts) is partial. Each join needs a title-specific source and an explicit note distinguishing appearance, mention, flashback, orbital setting or destruction where relevant. Gallery labels alone may not establish that distinction. Unjoined stories lack a verified relationship; reviewed gaps are recorded separately, not treated as “no locations.” Array order does not establish within-story travel order. Broad world labels cannot establish character trajectories.

Rogue One is a separately sourced catalog film with its own sequence; its Andor-to-A New Hope bridge is whole-work order. The Book of Boba Fett has seven separately sourced chapter records and its own narrative sequence; chapters 5–7 link the Mandalorian seasons. Revenge of the Sith is an included prequel film with its own progress ID; its Clone Wars connection remains an overlap annotation. Overlap is not an official scene-interleaving order. Film date caveats and unknown runtimes remain visible in record details.

Per-batch additions and caveats (Rebels, Bad Batch, Obi-Wan Kenobi, Solo and earlier) are recorded once in [release history](../../evidence/release-history.md).
