# Canon and source contract

[Base worlds](../../src/data.ts) and [journey worlds](../../src/journeyWorlds.ts) combine to 62 schematic destinations. The official appendix supports published system grids; setting and appearance sources support separate claims. Endor and Yavin 4 retain parent-system qualifications. A system grid does not establish an exact planet position or a story appearance.

Keep claim evidence distinct: screen appearance, parent system, published grid, and illustrative appearance are separate assertions. Published chronology must distinguish release order, story order, exact dates, and uncertain intervals. Future Canon/Legends and edition fields must not be inferred from names alone.

World descriptions are mild setting context, but the prototype is not certified spoiler-safe. Before expanding content, attach watched-title visibility rules to descriptions, relationships, thumbnails, and search results.

Conflicting or missing evidence remains explicit. Do not invent coordinates, routes, dates, or character appearances to complete the visual.

The [catalog](../../src/catalog.ts) exposes 193 release records. [Chronology](../../src/chronology.ts) separately supplies five whole-work viewing sequences for those IDs. Clone Wars follows its official table; Andor and Mandalorian preserve numbered narrative order, including flashbacks within their original episodes; the trilogy follows IV–V–VI. No exact BBY/ABY event calendar is asserted. The catalog’s original chronology flag describes the base release-only import, not the added sequence module.

The [relationship registry](../../src/connections.ts) is partial. Each join needs a title-specific source and an explicit note distinguishing appearance, mention, flashback, orbital setting or destruction where relevant. Gallery labels alone may not establish that distinction. Unjoined stories lack a verified relationship; reviewed gaps are recorded separately, not treated as “no locations.” Array order does not establish within-story travel order. Broad world labels cannot establish character trajectories.

Rogue One, Book of Boba Fett chapters 5–7, and Revenge of the Sith overlap are external references. They do not generate catalog/progress IDs unless separately imported. Overlap is not an official scene-interleaving order. Film date caveats and unknown runtimes remain visible in record details.
